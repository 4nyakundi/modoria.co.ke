OFFICIAL BRAND CASE STUDY // MODORIA LIMITED

R3 REALTORS LIMITED

REAL | REALTY | RELATIONSHIPS

R3 Realtors Limited is a Kenyan real estate company focused on the development of quality residential and commercial properties across Kenya. From carefully planned developments to thoughtfully designed living spaces, R3 seeks to create properties that combine quality, functionality, value, and long-term potential.

PHASE 01 // STRATEGIC DISCOVERY

Brand Purpose, Mission & Attributes

Brand Mission

To develop quality real estate that combines thoughtful design, strategic investment, and lasting value, while creating spaces where people can live, work, and thrive.

Brand Purpose

To transform opportunities in the Kenyan property market into well-designed developments that create tangible and lasting value.

PHASE 02 // LOGO ARCHITECTURE & GEOMETRY

The Mathematical & Symbolic Equation

Built Around 3 Fundamental Ideas

Properties, Trust and Growth: The architectural outer frame represents the company's foundation in real estate and property development.

The interconnected and continuous forms express trust, stability, and the relationships that R3 builds with its clients and partners. The triangles represent movements and progression, symbolizing development, expansion, and the pursuit of greater value.

PHASE 03 // COLOR & TYPOGRAPHY SYSTEM
